About KrishiVet

Behind every batch, a team of scientists.

At KrishiVet Nutra Solution, we are committed to transforming modern farming through advanced veterinary and agricultural nutrition. Our solutions are developed through Canadian research and applied to real farming conditions in Bangladesh, delivering measurable improvements in livestock health and farm productivity.

Our mission: research-backed nutrition for every farmer and veterinarian in Bangladesh — improving animal health and farm productivity through evidence, not marketing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers on safety, usage, ordering, and technical support — so farmers and distributors can use our products with confidence.

Products & usage

Are KrishiVet products safe for all livestock? To confirm

Yes. Products are formulated by veterinary and chemistry experts and tested for safety before release. Follow species and dosage guidance on labels and consult a veterinarian where needed.

How quickly will I see results? To confirm

Many farmers report visible improvements within 2 to 6 weeks of consistent use. Digestive and liver support often respond earlier; growth and reproductive outcomes may need a full production cycle.

Can I use multiple KrishiVet products at the same time?

Yes — products are designed for compatibility. For farm-specific planning, consult KrishiVet technical support or your local veterinarian.

Are your products suitable for organic farming?

Several products use natural ingredient bases. Contact us to evaluate suitability for your specific farm and certification requirements.

Quality & compliance

Where are KrishiVet products manufactured? To confirm

Formulations are developed by KrishiVet R&D Solution in Canada and manufactured under strict quality controls aligned with veterinary supplement safety standards.

Are the products registered with Bangladesh DLS? To confirm

KrishiVet is committed to full compliance with Department of Livestock Services regulations. Contact us for the latest registration status by product.

How should products be stored?

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and follow label-specific storage temperature instructions. Cold-chain integrity is maintained for temperature-sensitive formulations.

Ordering & distribution

How do I order KrishiVet products?

Order through authorized distributors, direct phone or email contact, or the website contact form. We can connect you to your nearest distributor.

Do you offer bulk or farm-pack sizes?

Yes — standard and bulk options are available for medium and large operations. Contact us for volume pricing.

Do you provide free samples?

Trial samples are occasionally provided via distributor networks and agricultural events. Veterinarians and larger farms can inquire directly with our team.
